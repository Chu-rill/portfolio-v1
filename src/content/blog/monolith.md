The simplicity of a well-structured monolith—one codebase, one deployable unit, fewer moving parts—can be overlooked in a world that is racing towards microservices.

Teams that split up too soon and chased microservices before their monolith ever worked have happened to me. The outcome? Ironically, there is less velocity along with increased complexity, latency, and overhead.

A monolith isn’t outdated, it’s underappreciated.

When done right, a monolith gives you:

Cohesive domain logic

Easier debugging

Faster local development

Simpler deployments

Less architectural regret

Microservices shine at scale, sure. But until then? A solid monolith is a superpower.

Build what works for now, not what you think you’ll need later. Scaling problems are good problems — they mean you're winning. Cross that bridge when you actually reach it.

Stay calm, stay focused, and let your architecture reflect your current reality, not your fantasy future.
